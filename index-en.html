Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for welcome packs and apparel?
We fulfill B2B orders starting from just 20–25 units for onboarding welcome packs, holiday gift sets, and custom branded hoodies, t-shirts, or backpacks. We tailor product selection to keep unit economics optimal for small and mid-sized IT and gamedev teams.
What branding and customization methods do you offer?
We focus on durable, retail-grade customization. For heavyweight apparel, we offer computer embroidery (flat and 3D puff), screen printing, and DTF transfers. For electronics, thermal bottles, and accessories, we use precision laser engraving and UV printing, paired with custom Pantone-matched gift boxes and sleeves.
How long does production take after approving the free mockup?
You will receive your free visual mockup in your brand colors within 24–48 hours. Once items and artwork are approved, production, branding, and custom kitting typically take 10–15 business days including delivery to your office in Warsaw.
Do you ship welcome packs and holiday gifts directly to remote employees?
Yes — in addition to bulk deliveries to offices in Warsaw and across Poland, we handle individual courier and parcel locker (Paczkomat) shipments directly to remote and hybrid team members across Poland and the European Union.
